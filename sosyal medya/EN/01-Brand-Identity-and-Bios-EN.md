## 🎙️ Brand voice (English)
- **Clear and simple.** No jargon. If you say "OCR," immediately explain "copy text from a scan."
- **Problem-first.** State the pain, then the fix.
- **Trustworthy, not hypey.** Avoid "the world's best." Be concrete: "runs on your device, your file never leaves."
- **Friendly but professional.** Minimal, purposeful emoji. No ALL-CAPS spam.
