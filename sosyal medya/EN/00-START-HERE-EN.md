## ⚠️ Honest targeting note (read this first)

Your website (`pdfplatform.app`) is currently **Turkish-first** for SEO (the prerendered landing content is Turkish). The app **UI itself supports English** (tr/en i18n), so English speakers can fully use every tool — but Google will primarily surface the Turkish version in organic search.

**What this means for English social media:**
- ✅ English social content is great for **reach, brand, and direct traffic** — people click your link and use the tools in English.
- ⚠️ It is **less connected to English organic SEO** right now, because there are no separate `/en/` landing pages.
- 💡 **Recommendation:** If you want to go hard on the English market, the highest-leverage next step is adding English SEO landing pages (`/en/tools/...`). Tell me and I can plan/build that. Until then, treat English social as a **direct-traffic + awareness** channel, not an SEO feeder.

**Decision:** Start with **one** English platform to test demand (I recommend **TikTok** or **YouTube Shorts** — both push content to new audiences regardless of follower count). If English engagement is strong, expand.

## 🧭 Golden rules (same as TR)

1. **Always add captions** — most people watch on mute.
2. **First 2 seconds = the hook.** State the problem immediately.
3. **Never show real personal documents** — always use a sample/dummy PDF.
4. **One tool, one message per post.**
5. **Every post gets the site link + CTA.**
6. **Privacy is your edge** — "your files never leave your device."
7. **Consistency beats perfection.**
