Best Free PDF Tools in 2026: Match the Tool to the Job

The best free PDF tool depends on the task. This guide groups the jobs into five categories and tells you what to look for in each.

There are hundreds of PDF tools online, but which one for which job? This guide sorts PDF work into five groups and shows what to look for in each, including privacy, watermarks and limits.

1. Editing and organizing

Merging, splitting, deleting, rotating and reordering pages is the most common need. These jobs are light, and in good tools they run on your device in the browser, so the file does not go to a server and the result is instant.

Merge PDF — Combine multiple PDFs into one, on your device.

2. Converting

Turning a PDF into Word, Excel or PowerPoint, or back, and getting a PDF as JPG. These are heavy tasks usually done on a server. The main criterion for a converter is how well it preserves layout, tables and fonts.

PDF to Word — Convert a PDF into an editable Word document.

3. Optimizing and securing

Reducing file size, adding or removing a password, adding a watermark or page numbers. Compressing a file to email it and encrypting before sharing are the common cases.

Compress PDF — Make your PDF small enough to email.

4. PDF with AI

Summarizing long documents, asking a document questions, extracting data from invoices or comparing two contracts. In AI tools the file itself usually is not uploaded; text is extracted and only the text is processed.

Summarize PDF — Summarize a long document in seconds.

5. Four checks before you choose

  • Privacy: is the file uploaded to a server or processed on your device? For sensitive documents, prefer on-device processing.
  • Watermark: does the free output get a forced watermark?
  • Signup: does the tool force an account for a simple task?
  • Limits: up to how many MB, and how many operations a day?
