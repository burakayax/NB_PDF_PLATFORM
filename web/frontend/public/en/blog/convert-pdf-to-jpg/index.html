PDF to JPG or PNG: Pick the Right Format and Resolution

A PDF cannot go everywhere, but an image can. Here is how to convert pages cleanly, which format to pick and the side effects to expect.

To share a page on social media, place it on a web page or drop it into a slide, an image is far easier to use than a PDF. Converting the page to JPG or PNG is the most practical route.

Where it helps

  • Sharing a single page as an image on social media.
  • Adding a preview to a website or blog post.
  • Dropping a page into a presentation as a picture.
  • Quickly showing a page to someone who cannot open a PDF.

Three steps

  1. Upload the PDF: Add your document to the PDF to JPG tool.
  2. Pages become images: Each page is processed as a separate high-resolution JPG or PNG.
  3. Download: Take the images one by one or all together as a ZIP.

JPG or PNG?

JPG is smaller and ideal for photo-heavy pages. PNG keeps sharp edges and crisper text and supports transparency, so prefer it for logos, diagrams and text-heavy pages.

Resolution cannot be fixed afterwards

Converting a page turns a vector document into pixels, and the resolution you choose decides the quality with no way back, because an image made at low resolution cannot be sharpened later. Roughly, medium suits on-screen sharing, high suits presentations and the web, and the highest suits print.

Why a screenshot is not enough

A screenshot looks like the fastest option, but the result is limited to your screen's resolution and usually comes out soft. It also picks up the frame, the margins and the viewer's interface. Converting directly gives a clean output at the page's own resolution, with straight edges and crisp text.

Whole page, embedded image or just a region?

This tool converts the whole page. If you only want a photo or logo inside the PDF at original quality, extract the embedded images instead of rasterizing the page, and there is no quality loss.

Often what you need is a table, chart or paragraph on the page rather than the page. Snipping the area directly is faster than converting and cropping in an image editor, and it preserves resolution.

Notes for social media and the web

  • A portrait A4 page is cropped in most social feeds, so snip the section you want first.
  • Compressing an image before putting it on a web page noticeably improves load time.
  • PNG works better for text-heavy pages and JPG for photo-heavy ones.
  • Text in a converted page is no longer text, so search engines cannot read it. Write important wording on the page as well.

A side effect to know about

When a page becomes an image its text layer disappears. It can no longer be copied, searched or read by screen readers. Sometimes that is what you want, but where accessibility matters or the document will be archived, keep the PDF itself and use the image only as a sharing copy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pages can I convert?

There is no hard page limit. Since each page becomes its own image, long documents arrive as an archive file. If you only need a few pages, splitting first is more practical.

Can I get a transparent background?

PNG supports transparency, but if the PDF page has a white background the output is white too. For transparency you need to process the image separately in an editor.

Why are the image files bigger than the PDF?

A PDF stores text as vectors and takes very little space, while an image records every pixel. So the image of a text-heavy page can be larger than its source.

Does converting a scanned PDF to an image make sense?

A scanned page is already an image, so converting it again adds a second round of loss. For such documents, extracting the embedded images directly gives a better result.

How do I convert a PDF to JPG?

Upload the PDF to the tool and each page is converted into a separate JPG or PNG image. Download them individually or as a ZIP.

Will image quality drop?

Pages are processed at high resolution for crisp results on screen and in print. For sharper text, prefer PNG.

Can I convert just one page?

Since each page becomes a separate image, you can pick and download the image for the page you want.
