How to Write and Mark Up a PDF: Highlight, Note and Draw

Reviewing a document, you want to highlight what matters, jot a note in the margin or box a clause. Markup PDF lets you add highlights, drawings, boxes, arrows and text notes — without erasing the text and without your file leaving your device.

Reviewing a contract, an assignment or a report, you want a pen in hand: highlight the key sentence, jot a note, box a clause, point with an arrow. Markup PDF lets you do this in the browser, with your file never leaving your device.

Which markup tools are there?

  • Highlighter: paints over text semi-transparently; the text below stays readable.
  • Pen: freehand drawing, with a straight-line mode too.
  • Box and Arrow: frame a region or point to it.
  • Text: write a note in the margin or on the document.

Step by step: mark up a PDF

  1. Open the PDF: Drag and drop or choose the document you want to mark up.
  2. Pick a tool and color: Highlighter, pen, box, arrow or text; then choose a color and thickness.
  3. Draw on the page: Drag to mark. Marker/pen offer a «Straight» mode to draw cleanly over lines of text.
  4. Edit and download: Use «Select» to move, resize or recolor a mark; undo with Ctrl+Z. Then «Apply & download».

Marks are added ON TOP of the document; existing text is never removed or changed. Highlighter and pen are semi-transparent, so the text below stays readable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I write on / mark up a PDF?

Upload the PDF to the Markup tool, pick highlighter/pen/box/arrow/text and drag on the page. Click «Apply & download» to get the marked-up PDF. Everything runs in your browser.

Does highlighting over text erase it?

No. Marks are added on top; existing text is not removed. Highlighter and pen are semi-transparent, so the text below stays visible.

Can I undo or edit a mark I made?

Yes. Undo with Ctrl+Z; use the «Select» tool to move, resize or recolor a mark afterwards.
