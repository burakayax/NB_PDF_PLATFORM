How to Save Original Images From a PDF

Converting a page to a picture and pulling out the pictures inside it are two different operations with very different results. Here is when to use which.

A PDF has a photo or logo you want as its own file. A screenshot gives you a soft, screen-sized copy. The better route is to take out the image exactly as it was embedded, at its original resolution.

Two jobs people mix up

  • PDF to image takes a picture of the whole page as JPG or PNG. Text, lines and photos are flattened together into one image.
  • Extract images does not render the page. It pulls out the embedded image files themselves, in their original format and resolution.

Need the whole page as a picture? Use PDF to Image. Need just the photos or logos at original quality? Use Extract Images.

Option 1: screenshot

It is the best-known method and the weakest. The result is capped by your screen resolution, edges are hard to crop cleanly and the original quality is gone.

Option 2: convert the page

If the picture fills the page, a high-resolution page conversion can work. If the page also holds text and other elements, everything merges into one image and you cannot isolate the photo.

PDF to Image — Download whole pages as high-resolution JPG or PNG.

Option 3: extract the embedded images

This returns the real image streams from inside the PDF, which is what the Extract Images tool does:

  1. Upload the PDF: Choose a document that contains photos or logos. If it is encrypted, enter its password.
  2. Click Extract Images: Each page is scanned and every embedded image is pulled out in its original format (JPG or PNG). An image used on several pages is saved once.
  3. Download the ZIP: All extracted images arrive in one ZIP archive, in a single click.

Extract Images from PDF — Upload a PDF and get every photo and logo inside it, without quality loss.

Typical uses

  • Reusing photos from a presentation or report.
  • Recovering a company logo from an old PDF at original quality.
  • Bulk-saving product images from a catalogue or brochure.

Privacy

Extraction runs on a server, but your file is not stored permanently and temporary data is cleared when the job finishes. Most of our other tools, such as merge, rotate and delete pages, run entirely in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I extract images from a PDF?

Upload the PDF to the Extract Images tool and click Extract Images. All embedded photos and logos are bundled into a ZIP in their original format and downloaded.

Is converting a page to an image the same as extracting images?

No. Converting a page takes a picture of the whole page. Extracting images pulls out only the actual embedded photo and logo files at original quality.

What format are the extracted images in?

Each image is extracted losslessly in the original format it was stored in the PDF, usually JPG or PNG.

Can I extract images from an encrypted PDF?

Yes. Enter the password and the PDF is unlocked for the operation so the embedded images can be extracted.
