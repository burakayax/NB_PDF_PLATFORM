Prepare Dozens of Invoices for Accounting (Batch)

Month-end is here, you have dozens of PDF invoices, and you need to enter them all into accounting. Here's how batch processing cuts it to minutes.

For small businesses and freelancers, month-end is spent opening PDF invoices one by one and entering them into an accounting sheet. Batch processing handles this in one step, without opening each invoice separately.

What does batch solve?

Processing one invoice is easy; the real pain is dozens in a row. AI Batch processes a whole folder of invoices in turn, extracts each one's data (invoice no, date, seller, amount, VAT) and merges them into one CSV table — one row per invoice.

  1. Bulk-upload invoices: Add your PDF invoices to the AI Batch tool (up to 25 files).
  2. Choose "Extract" mode: Start; you see live progress per invoice.
  3. Download one CSV: All invoices' data merge into one table; open in Excel and import into accounting.
