The Fast Way to Understand a Contract in a Foreign Language

Got a contract in a foreign language and need to grasp it fast? Here's how translation and summary work together.

In business you often face contracts, proposals or technical docs in a foreign language. Instead of translating every line with a dictionary, you can translate the document without breaking its structure and extract its key points.

Translate first, then summarize

The most efficient approach is two steps: (1) translate the document into your language, (2) if it's long, summarize the key points. On PDF Platform you can do both — translation preserves structure (headings, lists, tables); the summary surfaces parties, dates and obligations.

  1. Translate the document: Upload the PDF to AI Translate, pick a target language; it translates preserving structure and you can download a PDF.
  2. Summarize if long: Use AI Summarize to extract the key points, parties and critical clauses of the translated document.
  3. Ask about tricky parts: Use AI Chat to ask directly about a specific clause you want to understand.
