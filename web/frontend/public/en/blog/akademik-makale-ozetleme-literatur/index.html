Quickly Summarize Academic Papers (for Literature Review)

In a literature review, most time is lost screening out irrelevant papers. Use AI to tell in seconds whether a paper is useful.

For a thesis, assignment or research, reading dozens of papers one by one is nearly impossible. The real skill is quickly screening which paper is useful to you.

What to check quickly in a paper

  • Research question / hypothesis: what does it ask?
  • Method: what kind of study (experiment, survey, meta-analysis)?
  • Findings: what did it find?
  • Conclusion and contribution: what does it add, what are the limitations?
