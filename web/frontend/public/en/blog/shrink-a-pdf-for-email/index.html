How to Shrink a PDF to Email or Send on WhatsApp

Getting a "file too large" error? The easiest way to get a PDF under email and WhatsApp limits — step by step.

You want to email a PDF but it says "file too large", or WhatsApp won't accept the document. The fix isn't splitting it — it's compressing: reduce the size while keeping the content.

Why so big? And typical limits

  • Scanned documents and image-heavy PDFs take the most space.
  • Gmail attachment: ~25 MB · Outlook: ~20 MB · WhatsApp document: ~100 MB, but very large files stall in practice.
  • Portals and application systems often cap at 2–10 MB.

Shrink a PDF — step by step

  1. Upload the PDF: Drag and drop your file into the compress tool.
  2. Pick a compression level: At a balanced level there's no noticeable quality loss; a higher level reduces size further.
  3. Download and send: Download the optimized file — now under the email/WhatsApp limit.

Does compressing hurt quality?

At a balanced level, no — text stays selectable and crisp, and images stay readable. The text layer is preserved, so you can still search and copy in the compressed PDF. The biggest savings usually come from scanned, image-heavy PDFs.

Still too big? Remove unneeded pages with "Split/Delete", or send only the pages you need. If you're sending several documents as one file, compress first, then combine with "Merge PDF".

Know the real limit before you send

Whether a file is «too large» depends on where you are sending it, and most people learn the limit after hitting it. Email attachments also grow by roughly a third when encoded, so a 25 MB file often will not pass a 25 MB limit. Aiming a little under the limit is the practical habit.

Options besides compressing

  • Delete unnecessary pages, especially blank reverse sides in scans.
  • Send only the relevant page range; enough for the recipient to do their job is enough.
  • Split the document into sensible parts and send two messages.
  • For very large files, sharing a cloud link is more reliable than attaching.

Notes specific to WhatsApp

WhatsApp does not compress documents; it sends them as they are, which is good because the document is not degraded. But if you send a photo as an image, the app shrinks it and the paperwork can become unreadable. When sharing documents, converting the photo to PDF and sending it as a document preserves quality and looks more professional.

Checking before and after

The effect of compression is invisible at normal screen scale. After downloading, open the page with the smallest type and zoom in; if the letters read cleanly, the file is usable. Do not skip this on documents going to an institution — an illegible scan is one of the most common reasons applications come back.

Do not compress repeatedly

Compressing the same document a second and third time costs a little more quality each round without meaningful size gains. Reaching the target in one pass is best. If you cannot, the problem is the content rather than the compression: with many high-resolution scans, splitting or removing pages is the real solution.

Keeping it small from the start

  • Scan text documents at 300 DPI; 600 DPI inflates the file for no benefit.
  • Scan black-and-white paperwork in greyscale rather than colour.
  • Run phone photos through the Document Scanner instead of converting straight to PDF.
  • Review the page count before sharing — that is where the easiest gains are.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I search a compressed PDF?

Yes. The text layer is untouched, so the document stays searchable and copyable; the savings come from images.

What if it still will not send after compressing?

The limit may not be file size alone; some systems cap total message size or the number of attachments. Splitting the document helps there.

Is compressing a signed document a problem?

On documents with a digital signature, the content changes and the signature can become invalid. Clarify whether the signature is needed before compressing.

How much smaller will it get?

It depends on the content. Scanned, image-heavy documents shrink substantially; text-only documents are already small, so gains are limited.

How do I shrink a PDF to fit an email?

Upload it to the compress tool, pick a level, and download. The file drops under Gmail/Outlook limits (≈20–25 MB); for even smaller, choose a higher level.

Why won't WhatsApp send my PDF?

Usually because the file is too large. Compress the PDF to reduce its size and WhatsApp will send it as a document with no problem.

Does the text stay selectable after compressing?

Yes. The text layer is preserved, so you can still select, search and copy text in the compressed PDF.
