Why copy-paste translation falls short

When you copy text into a translation site, headings, lists and tables disappear; for long documents this becomes unmanageable. And numbers, dates and proper nouns need to stay intact.

When do you need a human translator?

AI translation is excellent for speed and comprehension. But for documents that need legal validity (notary, court, official applications), a sworn translator is required. AI is ideal for a fast first understanding and draft.
