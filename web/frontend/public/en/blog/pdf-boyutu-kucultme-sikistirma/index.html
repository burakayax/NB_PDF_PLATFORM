Reduce PDF File Size (Compress): Fit It Into an Email

Email saying "file too large"? PDFs usually bloat because of the images inside. This post shows how to reduce the size and what to watch for.

Most email services cap attachments at 20-25 MB. A scanned or image-heavy PDF easily exceeds that. The good news: most PDFs can be shrunk significantly without visible quality loss.

Why do PDFs get big?

Images dominate file size: high-resolution scans, photos and embedded images. Text takes very little space. So compression mainly works by bringing images down to a reasonable resolution.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reduce PDF size?

Upload the PDF to the Compress tool; it optimizes images to reduce size and you download the smaller file.

Does compression ruin quality?

The goal is the smallest size at a quality you won't notice. Scans shrink a lot with little visible loss.

Why is my PDF so large?

Usually high-resolution scans and photos. Compression brings those images down to a reasonable resolution.
