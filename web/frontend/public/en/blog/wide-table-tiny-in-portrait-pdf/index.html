Wide Table Tiny on a Portrait PDF Page? Make It Readable

The wide table sits as a thin strip in the middle of a portrait page with empty space above and below. How to enlarge it without retyping anything.

A payment schedule, account statement or wide table from a bank or an official screen is usually laid out landscape, but it gets printed to PDF on a portrait page. The result: the table is a thin strip in the middle of the page, with large empty areas above and below, and the text is hard to read.

Why does this happen?

The page orientation stays on portrait when printing or scanning. The wide content is fitted to the page width and shrinks; because it is not tall, unused space is left above and below. Rotating the page does not fix it either: the table then lies along the short side of the portrait page.

The fix: place the content on a landscape sheet

The «PDF to Landscape» tool finds the real content on the page, trims the empty margins and fits the content to the width of a landscape sheet. The text grows noticeably (about 1.3 times on a scanned payment schedule we tried). Nothing is retyped: in a scanned document the image itself is moved, in a digital document the text itself.

  1. Add the PDF: Drop the portrait PDF containing the table into the tool.
  2. Leave the layout on «Auto»: Because the content is wide, the page is fitted onto a landscape sheet.
  3. Press Make Landscape: The result is a landscape page with upright, larger text.
  4. Check the numbers: Open the original and the result side by side and compare a few rows.

Things to watch on scanned statements

  • The image's own resolution does not change. A blurry scan may look slightly soft when enlarged; it will not get sharper.
  • If you want clean, selectable text, run the result through the «Searchable PDF (OCR)» tool, and always check the digits in the OCR output.

Enlarging numbers without changing them is different from re-reading them and rebuilding a table. This tool does the first: it moves the image or the text as it is and does not regenerate it.

For digital (text) PDFs

In a digital table the text stays vector, so it is sharp when enlarged. If the table is too long for one sheet, the «Side by side» layout splits it into two columns along a table line; rows are not cut in half.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the table small on a portrait page?

Wide content is fitted to the portrait width, so it shrinks and leaves empty space above and below.

Does rotating the page help?

Usually not. The table lies on its side along the portrait page's short edge, and the text stays small.

Are the numbers retyped?

No. The image or text is moved as it is, not regenerated.

Will it sharpen a blurry scan?

No. The image's own resolution does not change.
