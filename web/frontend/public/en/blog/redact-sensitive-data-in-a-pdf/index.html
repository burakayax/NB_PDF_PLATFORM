Real PDF Redaction: Remove Personal Data Before Sharing

Drawing a black rectangle over text leaves the text in the file. Real redaction deletes it. Here is the difference, the fields people forget and a verification routine.

Before you share a contract, invoice or official document, personal data such as ID numbers, IBANs, phone numbers and addresses often has to be hidden, frequently as a legal requirement under GDPR or KVKK. But drawing a black box over text is not real hiding.

Why a black box is not safe

In most tools you hide sensitive data by drawing a black rectangle over it. The original text stays inside the document, so anyone who opens the PDF in a text editor, removes the box or copies the text can read it back. That is a leak and a compliance risk.

Real redaction removes the data from the document's content. Our redaction tool deletes the data entirely instead of covering it, so in the redacted spot searching, copying or recovery is impossible.

  1. Upload the PDF: Add your document to the redaction tool.
  2. Sensitive fields are found: Patterns such as ID, IBAN, phone and email are detected on your device, and context-based data such as names and addresses is detected with AI.
  3. Remove permanently: The fields you confirm are truly deleted from the document's content. Download the cleaned PDF.

When you need it

  • Before sharing a contract or invoice with a third party.
  • In official applications, tenders or court files.
  • For documents published as samples or templates.

Fields people overlook

Personal data does not sit only where you expect. When you check the middle of a page before sharing, the details around the edges get skipped, and that is exactly where leaks come from.

  • Names, branch codes or usernames in headers and footers.
  • The signature block and direct phone number of whoever prepared the document.
  • Records belonging to third parties left in annex table rows.
  • Readable details underneath stamp and signature images.
  • A customer number on an invoice, or a full account number on a statement.

Metadata is part of the document

Even with the page cleaned, a document can carry invisible information: the name of whoever created the file, the software used, creation and modification dates, and sometimes a title left from an earlier draft. These sit in the document properties and anyone curious can see them in a couple of clicks. Clearing metadata is the companion step to redaction on documents going outside.

What to hide and what to leave

Over-redacting makes the document useless, and under-redacting leaves the risk in place. The test is the purpose of sending it: which information does the recipient genuinely need to do their job? A lease sent for a loan application needs the rent figure, but usually not the landlord's identity number. Ask that field by field and you end up with a document that is both compliant and usable.

Verify afterwards

  • Reopen the document and search for what you removed. Any result means removal did not happen.
  • Select the text and paste it elsewhere; the redacted part should not appear.
  • Review every page, because the same detail may repeat elsewhere.
  • Check document properties, since a name or file path may remain in the metadata.
  • Save the redacted version under a new name and share from that file.

Scans behave differently

In a scanned document the page is an image. With no text layer there is no text to remove, and covering an area stays permanent on the image. But if the document has been through OCR, an invisible text layer sits beneath it, and the information in that layer can still be copied. With scans, check whether a text layer exists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can redacted information be recovered?

With permanent removal the text is taken out of the document, so it cannot be copied, searched or recovered. With methods that only draw a box, the information stays in the file and can be read back.

Does this alone make me compliant?

It removes the data technically, but compliance is not only technical; which data you share and why also matters. Use the tool as part of your compliance process.

Should I review the whole document?

Yes. Automatic detection catches known patterns, but something specific to your document, such as a name inside free text, can be missed. The final check is always a human one.

Does redaction break the document's appearance?

The removed area appears blank or covered, while the layout and the rest of the content stay as they were. Page count and formatting are preserved.

How do I redact sensitive data in a PDF?

Upload the PDF. The tool finds ID, IBAN, phone and email on your device and name and address with AI, then permanently removes them with your confirmation.

Is the data truly deleted, or just covered?

Truly deleted. The data is removed from the PDF's content, no box is drawn over it and it cannot be read back.

Is this enough for GDPR or KVKK?

Real redaction removes the personal data from the document, which makes it far safer than a black box. It is still recommended to review the result before sharing.
