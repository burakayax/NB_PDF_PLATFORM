PDF Watermark Guide: Wording, Opacity and Limits

A watermark is a quick way to label status and ownership. It is a deterrent, not a lock, so the wording and the settings matter more than the stamp itself.

A watermark lays a semi-transparent line of text over every page to show the document's status (draft, confidential, copy) or its owner. It is useful when you share contracts, send quotes or want people to think twice before passing a file on.

What it is good for

  • Stating status: DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, NOT APPROVED.
  • Stamping ownership or brand: your company name or website.
  • Discouraging sharing: the semi-transparent text stays visible even in screenshots.

Four steps

  1. Upload the PDF: Choose the document you want to watermark.
  2. Type the text: Enter wording such as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or your company name.
  3. Set colour, font and opacity: Pick the colour (#RRGGBB), the font and an opacity that keeps the document readable.
  4. Preview and download: Check the preview and, if it looks right, download the watermarked PDF.

Too strong and the text is hard to read; too faint and it is barely visible. An opacity of 10 to 20 percent works for most documents.

Choosing the wording

A watermark works only as well as it is clear. A page stamped Copy says little. One stamped Copy, 12 Mar 2026, Accounting tells you where it came from if it leaks. Three approaches are common in business:

  • Status: DRAFT, NOT APPROVED or CANCELLED signals that the document is not in force.
  • Confidentiality: CONFIDENTIAL, INTERNAL or DO NOT DISTRIBUTE reminds the reader of their duty.
  • Traceability: the recipient's name or a date shows which copy a leak came from.

Keep it readable

The most common mistake is making the document unreadable. A diagonal placement crosses fewer lines than a horizontal one and tires the eye less. Check at 100 percent zoom and, if you can, in print, because a watermark that looks faint on screen can print darker than you expect.

A deterrent, not protection

A watermark does not stop anyone copying, printing or forwarding the document. It only makes clear that whoever shared it did so knowingly. For real protection, encrypt with a password. The two do not replace each other and are often combined on sensitive files: the watermark reminds people of their duty, and the password limits who can open it.

  • To password-protect: Encrypt PDF.
  • For page numbers: Add Page Numbers.
  • If you need a signature: Sign PDF.

Keeping versions apart

When five versions of one contract circulate, working out which is current gets hard, and signing the wrong one is a real risk. Marking drafts with a watermark that carries the version and date largely prevents that. When the final version is ready, produce a clean copy from the source rather than trying to strip the watermark out.

Before you stamp

  • Is the content final? The layout will not shift, but changed content means starting again.
  • On multi-page documents the watermark applies to every page, so keep the cover separate if you do not want it there.
  • Pick a colour that does not clash with the document's own colours.
  • Save the watermarked version under a new name and keep the original clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a watermark be removed later?

Because it is baked into the document, removing it is not simple and can leave traces. The right method is to regenerate a clean copy from the source, which is why keeping a clean, unstamped version matters.

Is the watermark added to every page?

Yes, it is applied to all pages. If you want it only on some, split the document, watermark the relevant part and merge it back.

Does a watermark make the document unsearchable?

No. The existing text layer is untouched. The watermark is added over it and the document stays searchable.

Can I use an image watermark such as a logo?

This tool stamps text watermarks. Writing your brand name as text is enough for most purposes and stays more legible.

How do I add a watermark to a PDF?

Upload the PDF to the Watermark tool, type the watermark text, for example DRAFT, choose colour, font and opacity, preview and download.

Can I change the watermark colour and opacity?

Yes. You can set the text's colour (#RRGGBB), font and opacity so the document stays readable.

Does a watermark protect the document?

A watermark marks the document visually but does not prevent editing. For real protection, encrypt the PDF with a password.
