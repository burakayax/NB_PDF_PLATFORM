Fill In a PDF Form Without Printing, Even With No Fields

Most PDF forms you receive are flat, with nothing to click. Here is a thirty-second way to tell, and how to fill each kind cleanly.

Applications, contracts, petitions: most PDF forms are flat, with no ready fields to click and type into. You do not need to print and fill them by hand. You can put text directly on top of the page.

Which kind of form do you have?

Open the PDF and click a blank. If a cursor blinks and you can type, the form is fillable. If nothing happens, it is flat.

  • Fillable (interactive) form: it has ready fields that you click and type into in any PDF reader. Filling the fields directly is the cleanest route because the text sits inside the field and aligns itself.
  • Flat form: most official and scanned documents are like this. There are no ready fields, so you add text on top of the page at the right spot.

Filling a flat form in three steps

  1. Upload the form: Upload your PDF form to the annotate and text tool.
  2. Add text: With the text tool, click the relevant blank and type your answer. Add a separate box for each field.
  3. Sign and download: If needed, add your signature and download the filled PDF with Apply and download.

Add Text and Markup to PDF — Upload your form, add text to the blanks and download the filled PDF for free.

For checkboxes, add a small X or tick as text. If the form has several pages, add text and marks to each page separately.

Alignment on flat forms

  • Set the text size close to the line height; oversized text spills onto the neighbouring line.
  • Use a separate text box for each field, because one long line breaks the alignment.
  • A small mark is enough for a checkbox, so do not overflow the box.
  • Where day, month and year have separate boxes, type into each one separately.
  • Check at 100% zoom before downloading, since text that looks right at screen scale can shift in print.

Accented characters in fillable forms

Some PDF forms are built with a limited character set, so letters outside it can disappear or turn into question marks when typed. That is the form author's font choice, not a mistake by whoever fills it in. Reading the fields back once after filling is the practical way to catch it.

Why lock a fillable form

A fillable form sent as it is stays editable, so the recipient can change a field without noticing, and the form may look empty in a different program. Locking the fields makes what you typed a permanent part of the page, so the document looks the same everywhere. On an official application this ensures the form arrives complete and unaltered.

Signing and sending

If the form asks for a signature, draw or type yours after filling and place it. Signing happens entirely on your device and the document is not sent to a server. Sending one file with the signature included is tidier than separate pages. If the other side must sign too, send the document out for signature by email and keep a record of the process.

A final check

Are all required fields filled, is the date in the requested format, and are the requested attachments included? Those three questions prevent most applications from coming back. Keep a copy of the completed form too, which removes the later question of what you wrote.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change the fields after filling the form?

Fields stay editable unless you lock them. Once locked, changing something means starting again from the blank original, so keep the empty form.

Can I fill it on my phone?

Yes. Since it runs in the browser it works on a phone too, and zooming in helps you check alignment on a small screen.

Can I fill in a scanned form?

Yes. A scanned form has no ready fields, so you place text on top of the page. If the scan is skewed, rotating it first makes alignment much easier.

Is my file uploaded?

Form filling runs on your device and the document is not sent to a server. That matters on forms carrying identity and application details.

Can I fill a PDF that has no fillable fields?

Yes. On flat forms you add text directly on top of the form, so no printing is needed.

Is filling a PDF form free?

Yes. Upload your form, add text, and download the filled PDF.

Can I sign the form I filled?

Yes. After adding text you can draw and place your signature. Signing happens on your device and the file is not uploaded.

Can I fill the form on my phone or tablet?

Yes. The tool is browser-based and works on any device, including Android, iPhone and iPad.
