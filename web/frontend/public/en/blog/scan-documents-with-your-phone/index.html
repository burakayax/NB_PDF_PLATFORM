Scan a Document With Your Phone Camera and No App

Most scanner apps want an account, show ads or upload your files. A browser-based scanner does the same job on your device, so the page never leaves your phone.

You have a paper document and you need it as a PDF quickly. The usual route is to install a scanner app and accept the ads, the sign-up screens and the permissions that send your files to the cloud. There is another way: do the whole job in your phone's browser, with nothing to install and nothing uploaded.

What changes when it runs in the browser

An app takes space, needs updates, and most scanner apps send your document to their servers for processing. The PDF Platform scanner opens in your phone browser, and edge detection, perspective correction and PDF creation all happen on your device. You get no install, and your document is never uploaded.

Four taps

  1. Tap Scan document on the homepage: Open pdfplatform.app on your phone and tap the Scan a document with camera button above the tool picker. Allow camera access.
  2. Point at the document: The edges are marked live with a green outline. Hold steady and the system captures the page by itself, with no shutter press needed.
  3. Pick a filter and confirm the corners: Choose Color, Gray or Black and White. The detected corners are ready, and you can fine-tune them with your finger.
  4. Create the PDF: Add one page or several, then tap Create PDF. You can save it, share it or open it in the PDF tools.

Edge detection is fully automatic. Frame the document and hold still. With good light and clear contrast the edges snap instantly, and if not, drag the corners to adjust.

Several pages, one PDF

Scan every page of a contract or notebook back to back and they merge into a single PDF. Each page shows as a small preview, so you can check the order and remove any you do not want. The free plan scans up to 3 pages per scan, and Pro removes the limit.

Image to PDF — Already have photos? Turn them into one PDF in seconds, on your device and with no sign-up.

Privacy

The biggest worry when scanning IDs, contracts or invoices is the file going to a server. That does not happen here. The camera frame, edge detection and PDF creation all run in your browser, and the finished PDF goes only where you save or share it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an app to scan documents with my phone?

No. Open pdfplatform.app in your phone browser and tap Scan document. No install, sign-up or app store needed.

Do I have to select the document edges myself?

No, edges are detected automatically. Point the camera at the document and hold steady, and the system captures it by itself. You can adjust the corners by hand if needed.

Is my scanned document uploaded to a server?

No. Scanning, edge detection and PDF creation all run on your device, and your document is never sent to the internet.

Can I make one PDF from several pages?

Yes. Scan pages back to back and merge them into one PDF. The free plan allows 3 pages per scan, and unlimited pages is a Pro feature.
