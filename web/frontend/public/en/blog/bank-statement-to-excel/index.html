Bank Statement PDF to Excel: Get Clean Transaction Columns

Copying a statement out of a PDF scrambles the columns. Here is how to extract the transactions cleanly, verify them and import them without regional-format surprises.

Month-end bookkeeping, expense tracking and budgeting all start with the same chore: getting transactions from a PDF statement into a spreadsheet. Banks do not issue PDFs as clean tables, so copy and paste breaks the alignment.

Why the PDF resists

Rows look tidy on the page, but there is no table structure behind them. Copied dates, descriptions and amounts land in one cell or the wrong columns. AI recognizes the structure and divides it into the right columns.

Three steps

  1. Upload the statement PDF: Add it to the Extract Data tool. Text is read on your device.
  2. Click Extract Data: The AI turns transactions into a structured table of date, description and amount.
  3. Download the CSV: Open it in Excel, where you can filter and total.

Check whether your bank already offers CSV

Online banking often offers formats other than PDF, and most people never notice. If you only need the numbers and your bank can export CSV or Excel directly, that is the cleanest route. Often, though, a PDF is all you have: it came by email, from an archive, or the bank only offers PDF for older periods. This method is for that case.

Verify the extracted table

Do not feed unchecked data straight into your books. A few minutes of review costs far less than hunting for a balance that will not reconcile at month-end.

  • Row count: does the number of transactions match the statement?
  • Totals: do credits and debits agree with the statement's summary section?
  • Date format: has the day and month order been swapped, especially on ambiguous dates like 01/02?
  • Decimal separator: comma or point, and whether amounts break when opened in Excel.
  • Sign: did debits come through as negatives, or in a separate column?

Opening a CSV without damaging it

Double-clicking a CSV can mangle amounts depending on your regional settings. A number with a decimal comma may turn into text or be mistaken for a date. Open Excel empty and import through Data, From Text/CSV, choosing the delimiter and column formats yourself. Once set up, repeat the same steps every month.

Many months, many accounts

A yearly breakdown means twelve statements. Processing them one by one and pasting results underneath each other is slow and error-prone, so run them together as a batch and collect one table. If you combine several accounts, add an account column to make filtering easy.

Protect what you share

A statement carries your account number and a picture of how you spend. Before sending it to an accountant or a third party, remove what they do not need, such as part of the account number, your address or unrelated periods, using the redaction tool. Covering text with a box is not enough, because the text underneath can still be copied.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with any bank's statement?

Layouts differ between banks, but as long as the transaction list has date, description and amount columns, the structure is recognized. Unusual layouts may need the output reviewed.

Can I use a scanned statement?

In a scanned statement the text is an image, so it needs OCR first. If the scan is not sharp, the risk of errors in amounts rises, which is why a digital PDF is always safer.

Is the balance column extracted too?

It can be, if the statement has one. For checking, though, comparing credit and debit totals against the statement's summary is more reliable than trusting the balance column.

Can I import the result into accounting software?

CSV output is compatible with the import format of most accounting packages. If the program expects a different column order, rearranging the columns in Excel is enough.

How do I export a PDF bank statement to Excel?

Upload it to the Extract Data tool. The AI splits transactions into date, description and amount columns, and you download the CSV.

Are transactions split into the right columns?

Yes. The AI recognizes the statement structure and places data in the right columns, with no copy-and-paste misalignment.

Is my data safe?

Your PDF's text is extracted on your device and only text is processed. The file itself is not uploaded.
