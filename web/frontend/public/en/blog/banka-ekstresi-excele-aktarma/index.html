Export a Bank Statement (PDF) to Excel

Typing hundreds of transactions from a PDF bank statement into Excel is painful. Here's how AI turns it into a tidy table.

For month-end accounting, expense tracking or budgeting, you need bank statement transactions in Excel. But banks' PDF statements don't come as a clean table; copy-paste breaks the alignment.

Why is a PDF statement hard to export?

Even if rows look organized in the PDF, there's no table structure behind them. So when you copy, dates, descriptions and amounts land in one cell or the wrong columns. AI recognizes the structure and splits it into the right columns.

Statement → Excel step by step

  1. Upload the statement PDF: Add your bank statement to the Extract Data tool. Text is read on your device.
  2. Click "Extract Data": The AI turns transactions into a structured table of date, description and amount.
  3. Download CSV: Download as CSV and open in Excel; now you can filter and total.
