PDF on Your Phone: Merge, Edit and Scan Without an App

You don't need to install a bunch of apps to work with PDFs on your phone. Merging, deleting pages, rotating and scanning documents can happen right in your phone's browser, without your file leaving the device.

When you need to quickly merge two PDFs or scan a document on your phone, you don't have to head to the app store for a bulky, ad-filled app. Most of these tasks run right in your phone's browser, on your device.

Tasks that run in your phone's browser

  • Merging and splitting PDFs
  • Deleting, rotating and reordering pages
  • Creating a PDF from images (photos)
  • Scanning documents with the camera (edge detection + perspective correction)

Why is no-install better?

Installing an app takes space, needs updates, and many free apps lock features or add a forced watermark to the output. In the browser, a link opens and the job is done. And because these structural tasks run on your device, your file isn't sent to the internet — speed and privacy together.

Merge on Phone — Merge two PDFs on your phone, no app needed.

Scanning a document with your phone

The Document Scanner uses your phone camera to automatically find the document's corners, correct the perspective and produce a clean PDF — entirely on your device. You can digitize IDs, contracts or invoices without sending them to the cloud.

Scan Document — Scan a document with your phone camera into a PDF — on your device, private.

What about heavy jobs?

Heavy jobs like Word/Excel conversion, OCR and compression aren't practical on a phone, so they run on the server; they work on mobile too, the file is just uploaded. Structural tasks (merge, scan, etc.) stay entirely on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an app to merge PDFs on my phone?

No. Merging, deleting/rotating pages and scanning documents run right in your phone's browser, on your device; you don't need to install an app.

Is my file safe on my phone?

Structural tasks (merge, split, rotate, scan) happen entirely on your device; the file isn't sent to the internet. Only heavy jobs like conversion/OCR run on the server.

Is a watermark added to the output?

No. No forced watermark is added to the PDF you create.
