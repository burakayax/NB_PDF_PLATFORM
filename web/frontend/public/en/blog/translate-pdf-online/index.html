When do you need a human translator?

AI translation is excellent for speed and comprehension. But for documents that need legal validity (notary, court, official applications), a sworn translator is required. AI is ideal for a fast first understanding and draft.

To quickly grasp what a document is about before translating, you can first generate a summary with the AI Summarize tool.

Preparing the document before translating

Translation works on the text extracted from the document, so how cleanly that text comes out decides how accurate the translation is. In a scan, skewed pages and shadows lower recognition, and every word lost there is missing from the translation too. Straightening pages and making the document searchable first is the cheapest investment you can make in translation quality.

What should not be translated

A good translation does not translate everything. Some elements must stay as they are, because translating them costs them their function rather than their meaning.

  • Names of people, organizations and brands.
  • Addresses, especially if they will be used for international post.
  • Product codes, serial numbers and reference numbers.
  • Names of legislation and clause numbers.
  • Currency abbreviations and technical units.

Checking these first while reviewing a translation surfaces the easiest errors to catch.

Care with technical and legal texts

Field-specific terms can differ from their everyday meaning, and a translation without context misleads. In law, concepts such as indemnity, warranty and liability map differently between legal systems; in technical texts the same word is read differently by different industries. Use the translation to understand; when a binding decision is at stake, go back to the original text and, if needed, to a specialist.

Keeping the translation with the original

When working with a foreign-language document, the most useful arrangement is to keep the original and the translation together. Merged into one document, you can show the text the other side sees beside the text you read while discussing a clause. Working with a team, that is the most practical way to be sure everyone means the same thing.

What you can do after translating

  • Summarize a long document to pull out its key points.
  • Ask the document directly about a clause you do not follow.
  • Extract the difference if you have two versions.
  • Remove unnecessary personal data before sharing the translation.
