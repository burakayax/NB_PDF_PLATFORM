Fixing a PDF That Mixes Portrait and Landscape Pages

Your merged file ended up with mixed page orientations. Can you convert just the portrait ones and leave the landscape pages alone?

Merge files from different sources and one PDF can end up with portrait and landscape pages side by side. Scrolling on a screen or a projector, you tilt your head on some pages and look straight on others. If you want one orientation throughout, converting only the portrait pages while leaving the landscape ones alone is enough.

Where does mixed orientation come from?

  • A landscape table or chart page added to a portrait report.
  • PDFs from several sources merged into one file.
  • Scans where some pages came in sideways and others upright.

Converting only the portrait pages

The «PDF to Landscape» tool leaves a page untouched if it is already landscape. Only portrait pages move to a landscape layout and the page order does not change. Consecutive portrait pages are laid out as one flow in two columns; a landscape page in between breaks the flow and stays where it is.

  1. Add the PDF: Drop the mixed-orientation file into the tool.
  2. Pick a layout: «Auto» or «Side by side»; landscape pages are kept in both cases.
  3. Press Make Landscape: Portrait pages are converted and landscape pages stay where they were.
  4. Check the order: Confirm in the preview that the page order matches the original.

PDF to Landscape — Convert only the portrait pages of a mixed-orientation PDF.

Good to know

  • The page count can change: two portrait pages of content may fit on one landscape sheet.
  • A landscape page splits the flow of the portrait pages before and after it; those two groups are laid out as separate flows.
  • Empty pages are left as they are.
  • Form fields and links keep working in their new positions.

If you only want to change the orientation of every page, the «Rotate PDF» tool is enough. «PDF to Landscape» is for when you want the content placed on a landscape sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do landscape pages change too?

No. Pages that are already landscape stay as they are.

Does the page order change?

No. The order is kept and landscape pages stay in place.

Why can the output have fewer pages?

Because two portrait pages of content can fit on one landscape sheet as two columns.

Can I just rotate instead?

Yes, the «Rotate PDF» tool turns pages, but the content is not re-laid out onto a landscape sheet and the text lies sideways.
