Split a PDF: Separate a PDF into Pages or Parts (2026)

Need only a few pages from a thick PDF? Want to break a document into parts? Here's the easiest way to split a PDF — entirely in your browser, step by step.

Sometimes you don't need a whole document, just a few pages: the signature page of a contract, a single section of a report, or a small part to email. Splitting a PDF is exactly for that.

Common uses for splitting a PDF

  • Extracting only the pages you need from a thick document.
  • Breaking one PDF into sections to share separately.
  • Splitting a large file into smaller parts for an email attachment.
  • Saving each page as its own PDF.

Step by step: split a PDF

  1. Open the PDF: Drop your file into the "Split PDF" tool. Pages are listed in a preview grid.
  2. Select pages/range: Mark the pages you want. Selected pages can be combined into one PDF, or each saved as a separate file.
  3. Download: Download the result as a single PDF, or separate files inside a ZIP.

Split PDF — Pull out the pages you need — no signup, on your device, in seconds.

Split or delete?

If you want to keep just a few pages, splitting them out is the most practical. Conversely, if you want to keep most pages and drop a few, "Delete Pages" is faster. Both run on your device.

Tips

  • To recombine parts into one file, use the "Merge PDF" tool.
  • If pages are out of order, fix them first with "Organize pages".
  • Because it runs in your browser, your file is not sent over the internet.
