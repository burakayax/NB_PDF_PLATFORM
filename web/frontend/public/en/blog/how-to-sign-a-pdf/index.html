Sign a PDF Online: Draw, Type or Upload Your Signature

Printing, signing and scanning a contract wastes time and quality. Signing the PDF directly takes a minute, and nothing leaves your device.

Print, sign by hand, scan back: slow, and the result looks worse each time. Signing the PDF itself is faster and cleaner, and here it happens without an account and without your file leaving your device.

Visual signature or qualified signature?

In everyday use, signing a PDF means placing your signature image on the document. That is enough for most contracts, quotes, rental forms and letters. A qualified electronic signature (QES), which some authorities require, needs a separate certificate or device. This tool provides the first kind: quick, visible signature placement.

Three ways to create your signature

  • Draw: the most natural option with a finger on a touch screen.
  • Type: your name is turned into a handwriting font.
  • Upload: add your stamp or signature image; a transparent PNG works best.

Sign in four steps

  1. Open the PDF: Drag and drop or choose the contract or form. The file opens in your browser.
  2. Create your signature: Click Add signature to draw, type or upload it. It is placed in the centre of the page as soon as it is created.
  3. Place and adjust: Drag it into position, resize from the corner, rotate with the top handle and adjust opacity if needed. You can add a date or text too.
  4. Apply and download: For multi-page files use Apply to all pages to copy it to the same spot, then click Apply and download. Your signed PDF is ready.

Your signature and file are not uploaded. Signing happens entirely in your browser, on your device, so you can sign confidential documents safely.

A signature on every page

Multi-page contracts often need a signature or initials on each page. Place yours once and click Apply to all pages to copy it to the same position everywhere. You can also use Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V to copy an item elsewhere.

Add a date and a note

Next to the signature you can add a date and free text, for example 'Read and approved', choose any text colour and position everything by dragging. It is all written into the PDF in one pass.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign a PDF?

Upload the PDF and click Add signature to draw, type or upload it. It is placed on the page; drag to position it, resize from the corner, then click Apply and download. Everything runs in your browser.

Is my signature uploaded to a server?

No. Signing happens entirely on your device, and the PDF and signature are never uploaded. It is private and needs no sign-up.

Can I put the same signature on multiple pages?

Yes. Place it on one page and click Apply to all pages to add it at the same position across all pages.
