Reorder PDF Pages: Rearrange a Document Easily (2026)

Pages scanned in the wrong order? Want to move a page to the front or back? Here's the easiest way to rearrange PDF pages — entirely in your browser.

Documents that come out of a batch scanner in reverse, a page that needs to be inserted later, or just a more logical flow… reordering PDF pages is far easier than you might think.

When do you need to reorder pages?

  • Fixing pages a batch scanner delivered in reverse order.
  • Moving an annex or cover to the front/back of the document.
  • Tidying the flow after merging several documents.
  • Putting sections in a logical order before a presentation.

Step by step: rearrange pages

  1. Open the PDF: Drop your file into the "Organize pages" tool. Pages appear as numbered thumbnails.
  2. Set the new order: Move pages with the up/down buttons or type a target position number. You'll see the new order instantly.
  3. Download: Confirm and download the rearranged PDF. Your original file stays unchanged.
