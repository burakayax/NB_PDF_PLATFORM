Document Photo to Clean PDF: Straighten, Remove Shadows and Choose a Filter

A photographed page is slanted, has a desk behind it and a shadow on one side. Four corners and a lighting correction turn it into something that looks scanned.

Photographing a page is easy. Getting a result that looks like a scan is harder: the document is slanted, the desk is in frame and one corner is in shadow. That is exactly where a good scanner steps in and turns the photo into a flat, clean document.

Perspective correction

Because you rarely shoot perfectly top-down, the edges are not parallel and the page looks slanted. The scanner finds the four corners automatically and recomputes the image so the document becomes a flat rectangle, as if viewed head-on. The desk behind it is cropped away.

Shadow removal and contrast

Handheld shots rarely have even light, with one edge dark and another bright. The Auto filter estimates the background lighting on each colour channel and balances it, removing shadows and flattening the background toward white. Text sharpens and colours stay, much like the magic colour effect in scanner apps.

Which filter for which page

  • Color: invoices with logos and documents with photographs.
  • Gray: text-heavy documents, with a smaller file.
  • Black and White: the sharpest text and smallest file, ideal for plain text.
  • Auto (Pro): shadow removal plus contrast, for the most professional, scanner-like output.

Getting a good capture

  1. Good light, flat surface: Place the page on a flat surface and shoot from above without casting a shadow. Keep contrast between the surface and the page so the edges are easy to find.
  2. Wait for auto-capture: Hold the camera steady. The edges are marked with a green outline and the page is captured by itself.
  3. Pick a filter and create: Choose the right filter for the document and create the PDF, adjusting corners if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I straighten a document I shot at an angle?

Yes. The scanner finds the four corners automatically and corrects perspective. The document becomes a flat rectangle as if viewed head-on, with the background cropped.

How do I remove shadows from a document?

The Auto filter balances background lighting to remove shadows and flatten the background toward white. This filter is a Pro feature, while the Color, Gray and Black and White filters are free.

Which filter gives the smallest file?

Black and White gives the sharpest text and smallest file, and is ideal for plain text documents. For colour documents, prefer Color or Auto.
