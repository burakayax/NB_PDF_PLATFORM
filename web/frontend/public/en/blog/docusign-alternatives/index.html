DocuSign Alternatives for Small Teams, No Envelope Limits

DocuSign built the category, but it bills per envelope. If you send ten contracts a month the personal plan is spent by mid-month. Here are the numbers and the trade-offs.

For e-signatures, DocuSign is the benchmark, and it earned that. The catch is the billing unit: you pay by the number of documents sent, and the personal plan allows five a month. A one-person business sending ten contracts runs out around the middle of the month.

The pricing, from DocuSign's own page

DocuSign counts envelopes, meaning each package of documents sent for signature.

  • Personal: $11 per month (billed annually), 5 envelopes per month.
  • Standard: $30 per user per month, 100 envelopes per user per year.
  • Business Pro: $45 per user per month, 100 envelopes per user per year.

Note that even the higher plans stop at 100 envelopes a year, roughly eight a month. Past that, you are talking to sales.

What you give up by leaving DocuSign

  • Ready-made integrations with Salesforce, SAP, Google Workspace and others.
  • Qualified electronic signature options and certifications for regulated industries.
  • Bulk send, template management, in-person signing and other advanced flows.
  • iOS and Android apps and enterprise user management.

If you need any of these, switching saves nothing. The rest of this article is for people who do not.

The job most people are actually doing

For freelancers, small agencies, accountants, estate agents and sole traders the task is usually one sentence: send this contract, let the other party sign it, and keep a record that they did. Envelope quotas, integration catalogues and user management are not part of that sentence.

How a signature request works here

  1. Upload and address: Upload the document and enter the signer's email address.
  2. The signer opens their own link: Without creating an account they view the document, type their name, draw their signature and tick the consent box.
  3. You receive the signed original: The signature is applied to the original held on the server, and a certificate is appended.

The detail worth knowing: no file ever comes back from the signer. Whether the signed document is the one you sent is settled by how the system works, not by an audit after the event.

The appended certificate records:

  • When the request was created, sent, viewed and signed.
  • The signer's name and email address and the authentication method.
  • The moment consent was given.
  • SHA-256 fingerprints of the document before and after signing.

The link is valid for 14 days. A signer can decline, optionally giving a reason; you are told by email and it goes into the audit record.

Legal validity, stated plainly

This creates a visual electronic signature with the record of the process attached, the approach used for most commercial documents. A qualified electronic signature is a separate legal category that requires a certificate from a qualified trust service provider, and we do not offer one.

If the document needs notarisation, goes to a public authority, or the law specifically demands a qualified signature, this is the wrong tool. Use DocuSign's qualified options or a local trust service provider.

Cost side by side

  • DocuSign Personal: $11 per month for 5 documents a month.
  • PDF Platform Plus: $6.99 per month with 600 operations a month and all 34 tools. Signature requests are included from Plus upwards.

The two are not the same kind of product. DocuSign is a signing workflow; this is a document toolkit that includes signature requests. If signing is all you need, the second costs less. If you need signing infrastructure, the first gives you more.

Matching the tool to the situation

  • A few contracts a month with a record of the process: signature requests are enough, and cheaper.
  • A qualified signature or an industry certification: DocuSign or a local trust service provider.
  • CRM integration, bulk send or templates: DocuSign.
  • Only signing your own document: neither. The PDF signing tool is free, needs no account and runs on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does DocuSign have a free plan?

No free plan appears on their pricing page. Plans start at Personal ($11 per month, 5 envelopes per month), and the page does not say whether a trial is offered.

Does the signer need an account?

No. They open the link from their email, view the document, type their name, draw their signature and confirm. No account, install or payment.

How do I know the signed document was not altered?

The signature is applied to the original held on the server and no file is accepted back from the signer. SHA-256 fingerprints from before and after signing are recorded and printed on the audit certificate.

Is this signature legally valid?

It produces a visual electronic signature with a record of the process attached, the approach used for most commercial documents. A qualified electronic signature is a separate legal category requiring a certificate from a qualified trust service provider, which this tool does not offer.

Which plan includes signature requests?

Plus and above. They are not on the Free or Starter plans. Signing your own document, by contrast, is free and needs no account.

How long is the link valid?

14 days. After that the link stops working; you can cancel the request and send a new one.
