CamScanner Alternative: Free, Watermark-Free, Private

Scanner apps are handy, but many show ads, stamp the output or send your pages to their cloud. Here is an honest look at the alternative and its limits.

Apps such as CamScanner get the job done, but many ask for an account, watermark the free output, show ads and upload your documents to their own cloud for processing. When the pages are sensitive, that last point alone is unsettling.

What browser plus on-device changes

The PDF Platform scanner opens in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to install. More importantly, everything runs on your device: the camera image, automatic edge detection, perspective correction and PDF creation. Your document is never uploaded to a server, and no forced watermark is added to the output.

A plain comparison

  • Install: no app; it opens in your phone's browser.
  • Privacy: scanning happens on your device, and the document is not uploaded to the cloud.
  • Watermark: no forced watermark on the output.
  • Free: scanning, PDF creation, saving and sharing are free, with up to 3 pages per scan.
  • Pro: automatic shadow removal, unlimited pages and searchable PDF (OCR).

What the free plan covers

Scanning, automatic edge detection, perspective correction, Color, Gray and Black and White filters, PDF creation, saving and sharing are all free, and no account is needed. The limit is up to 3 pages per scan.

What Pro adds

For more professional scans Pro adds three things: an Auto filter that removes shadows and whitens the background, unlimited pages per scan, and searchable PDF (OCR), which makes text findable with Ctrl+F and copyable. All of it still runs on your device.

Image to PDF — Turn your documents or photos into a single PDF, on your device and with no account.

Who it suits

It suits anyone who scans now and then and does not want another app, and anyone who wants to digitize IDs, contracts and invoices without uploading them to a cloud. It opens from a link, does the job, and the document stays with you.
