About

Working on your documents should be fast, clear and safe. That is why we build PDF Platform.

Why we exist

We deal with PDFs every day: merging, compressing, signing, converting to Word, quickly understanding a contract. Too often that means uploading your file to a server you know nothing about, getting a cropped or watermarked result while you are still trying the tool for free, and then hitting a paywall.

PDF Platform is a web platform built by NB Global Studio to turn that experience around. Our aim is to offer PDF tools in Turkish and English, with a simple interface and a product that says plainly what it does.

Mission and vision

  • Our mission To make document work fast, clear and secure for everyone. We help our users save time with transparent tools that say plainly what they do, without moving files to servers unnecessarily.
  • Our vision To be the trusted document platform that makes privacy, simplicity and transparency the standard.

How we work

  • Your file stays on your device wherever possible Tools such as merge, split, rotate, delete and reorder pages, image to PDF and document scanning run without your file ever leaving your browser. For tools that need a server, the file is deleted as soon as processing ends. You can see, tool by tool, what happens where in the table in our Privacy Policy.
  • AI only runs when you choose it In AI tools such as summarize, translate and data extraction, only the text extracted from your document is sent to produce the result; the file itself is not sent and the text is not used to train models. AI output can contain mistakes, so please check it before relying on it for important decisions.
  • No surprises on the result screen We do not add visible watermarks to your output files. What is free and what needs an account is shown up front. Subscriptions never renew on their own: we remind you before the period ends and you decide whether to continue.
  • We listen to users You can rate every tool and tell us what is missing. New tools and improvements are shaped largely by that feedback.

What we offer

  • PDF tools Merge, split, compress, rotate, organize pages, convert, protect, sign and more.
  • AI-powered tools Document summary, chat with a document, translation, data extraction and contract review.
  • Document Scanner Turns a photo of a document taken with your phone into a straightened, cropped PDF.
  • CV and photo tools Build a CV from templates, write a cover letter and prepare application photos.
