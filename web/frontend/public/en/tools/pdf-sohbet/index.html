Frequently Asked Questions

How does chatting with a PDF work?

Upload the PDF and ask questions in plain language. The AI answers based only on the document; if the answer isn't there, it says so.

Are the answers reliable?

Answers come only from your uploaded document, with no fabrication — so you get accurate answers grounded in your file.

Does it work whatever language the document is in?

Yes. It works with documents in Turkish, English, and many languages, and you can ask in the language you prefer.

Are my document and chat kept private?

Your document is processed only to answer your questions; the chat session is yours.

What kind of questions can I ask?

Ask anything about the file in plain language — 'What's the contract term?', 'Is there a penalty clause?', 'What's the total amount?'
