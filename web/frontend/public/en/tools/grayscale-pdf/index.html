Grayscale PDF (Make Black-and-White)

Turn every colour of a PDF into grey. Text and drawings stay selectable; images become black-and-white. Ideal for saving ink and file size when printing.

How to use: Grayscale PDF (Make Black-and-White)

  1. Upload the PDF: Choose your colour PDF with «Choose file».
  2. Apply grayscale: Click the grayscale button. The file is uploaded for processing and deleted when it finishes.
  3. Check the result: Colours turn into shades of gray; text and drawings stay selectable.
  4. Download the PDF: Save the black-and-white copy.

When is it useful?

  • Saving printer ink: Convert a colour report to grayscale before printing it in black and white.
  • Official applications: Prepare a document for portals that ask for black and white.
  • Photocopy-style output: Give a document a plainer look when colour information is unnecessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a colour PDF black-and-white?

Upload your PDF and press the grayscale button; all colours on the page turn grey. Then download the file.

Does text stay selectable after grayscale?

Yes. Text and vector drawings are not turned into pictures; only their colours change, so text is still selectable and searchable.

Does the file get smaller?

Usually yes, because colour images are converted to 8-bit grey images. How much depends on how many images the document has.

Do all colours turn grey?

In most documents, yes. Some pattern, shading and ICC-profile drawings may keep their colour; check the result before downloading.

Do I need this to print in black-and-white?

Your printer's black-and-white setting also works, but making the PDF itself grey gives a consistent look when e-mailing or archiving too.

Does grayscale reduce image quality?

Only the colours change to gray; resolution is kept. Charts that rely on colour can be hard to read in grays, so check the result.

Can I apply it to specific pages only?

No, it is applied to all pages of the document.
