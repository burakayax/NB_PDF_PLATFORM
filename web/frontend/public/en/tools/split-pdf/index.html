Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split a PDF into separate pages?

Upload your PDF, select the pages or ranges to split, and download. Get each page as a separate file or a selected range as one file.

Can I extract a specific page range?

Yes. Select, for example, pages 5–10 and download only that range as a new PDF.

Can I split a PDF so every page becomes a separate file?

Yes. Use the 'each page as a separate file' option to split all pages into individual PDFs, downloaded together as a ZIP.

Does splitting upload my files to a server?

No. Splitting happens entirely in your browser — your PDF never leaves your device and stays 100% private.

Will the split PDFs lose quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly, so text, images, and formatting stay identical to the original.
