Add a QR Code to a PDF — Place It Anywhere

You don't need a design program to put a QR code on a business card, invoice, menu, brochure, certificate or invitation. Pick your PDF, enter the QR content (website, contact card, phone, email, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi or plain text) and drag the code to the spot you want on the page preview. Set its size, colour, margin and a short caption under it; add your logo in the middle if you like. The QR is drawn into the PDF as a VECTOR — it stays sharp in print — and when the content is a web address it becomes a clickable link inside the PDF. Your document's text, links and forms stay exactly as they were. Everything happens in your browser; your file is NEVER uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a QR code to a PDF?

Pick the PDF, enter the QR content, drag the code to where you want it on the preview and press 'Add QR to PDF'. The result downloads as 'original-name_qr.pdf'.

Is my file uploaded?

No. QR generation and placement happen entirely in your browser; your document never leaves your device.

What kinds of QR codes can I make?

Website link, contact card (vCard — saves to the phone's contacts when scanned), phone, email, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi and plain text. Accented characters are encoded correctly.

Will the QR look blurry in print?

No. It is drawn into the PDF as vector shapes, not an image, so it prints sharp at any size. You can also download the QR separately as PNG or SVG.

How small can the QR be?

We recommend at least 15 mm for reliable scanning; the tool warns you below that. If you add a logo, error correction is raised to the highest level automatically.

Which pages get the QR?

The first page, the last page, all pages, or specific pages such as '1,3,5-7'.

Do I need an account?

Yes, this tool is for members; a free account is enough. Your file still never leaves your device.

Can I put a different QR on each page?

Yes. Turn on 'A different QR on each page' and write 'pages | content' on each line, for example '1 | example.com/a' and '2,4-6 | example.com/b'.

How do I find free space for the QR?

'Suggest free spot' scans the page and shows the best corner that will not cover text or images. Ready-made templates for business cards, menus and invoices are included.
