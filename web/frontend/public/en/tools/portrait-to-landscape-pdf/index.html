Convert a Portrait PDF to Landscape Pages

Making a portrait PDF landscape is not always «rotate 90°»: rotate it and you read with your head tilted. This tool moves the page content onto a landscape sheet as it is, without retyping anything. Text-heavy documents flow into two columns, wide tables and scanned pages fill the landscape sheet. Letters and digits are not changed; only position and scale are.

How to use: Convert a Portrait PDF to Landscape Pages

  1. Upload the PDF: Choose the portrait PDF you want to make landscape with «Choose file».
  2. Pick the layout: «Auto» is best for most documents. Choose «Side by side» to flow content into two columns, or «Fit» to shrink the page onto a landscape sheet as it is.
  3. Press Make Landscape: Text, tables and images are placed on the landscape sheet without being retyped.
  4. Check and download: Review the result in the preview. The file is uploaded for processing and deleted when it finishes; save the landscape copy.

When is it useful?

  • Wide-table documents: Bring a bank statement, payment plan or table that is tiny on a portrait page up to a readable size on a landscape sheet.
  • Presenting and screen reading: Read a portrait report on a landscape screen or projector without tilting your head.
  • Mixed-orientation files: In a file that mixes portrait and landscape pages, convert only the portrait ones; landscape pages stay as they are.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from rotating a PDF?

Rotating turns the page by 90°; it is still a portrait page and the text lies sideways. This tool places the content on a landscape sheet so the text reads normally.

Can letters or numbers change?

No. The content is not retyped or re-created (no OCR, no AI). The page itself is moved; only position and scale change.

What do «Auto», «Side by side» and «Fit» mean?

«Side by side» flows the content into two columns, continuing where the previous column stopped. «Fit» places each page whole on a landscape sheet. «Auto» fits pages whose content is already wide and flows the tall ones into columns.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Yes. Scanned pages are placed the same way and cuts are made in the white space between text lines. The image's own resolution is unchanged, so a blurry scan does not become sharper.

What happens to form fields and links?

Fillable form fields and clickable links move to their new positions and keep working.

What about pages that are already landscape?

They are left untouched. In mixed-orientation documents only the portrait pages are converted and the page order is preserved.

Any issue with searching or copying text?

In side-by-side layout, if a page is split across two columns, that page's text exists in the file as two parts. It looks single on screen, but search or copy may return that page's words twice.

Which plans include this tool?

PDF to Landscape is included in the Plus, Pro and Business plans.

Is this different from rotating?

Yes. Rotating lays the page on its side; this tool places the content on a landscape sheet and the text reads normally.

Can digits or letters be wrong afterwards?

No. The content is not retyped; the page itself is moved and only position and scale change.
