Alternate & Mix PDF (Interleave Pages)

Interleave the pages of two (or more) PDFs. If your scanner saves a double-sided scan as separate fronts and backs, this tool merges them into one document in the right order.

How to use: Alternate & Mix PDF (Interleave Pages)

  1. Upload the two PDFs: Choose the file with the front sides first and the file with the back sides second.
  2. Set the order option: If you scanned the backs in reverse, turn on reading the second file in reverse order.
  3. Interleave: The files are uploaded for processing and deleted when it finishes. Pages are arranged as 1st front, 1st back, 2nd front, 2nd back…
  4. Download the single PDF: Save the document with its pages merged in the right order.

When is it useful?

  • Double-sided documents on a single-sided scanner: If you scanned all fronts and then all backs, turn them into one document in the right order.
  • Contracts and manuals: Scan a double-sided printed booklet and put its pages in order.
  • Alternating two sources: Interleave two documents' pages as A, B, A, B to read them side by side.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn a double-sided scan into one PDF?

Choose the PDF with the fronts first and the PDF with the backs second. If you scanned the backs in reverse order, tick 'Read the second in reverse'; the tool arranges pages as front 1, back 1, front 2, back 2…

How is alternate mix different from normal merge?

Normal merge adds all of the first document, then all of the second. Alternate mix takes pages one at a time: A1, B1, A2, B2…

I scanned the backs in reverse order. What do I do?

Tick 'Read the second in reverse' so the last page of the second document becomes the back of the first page.

What if the two documents have different page counts?

The remaining pages of the longer one are appended; no page is lost.

Can I interleave more than two PDFs?

Yes, up to 10 PDFs. Pages are taken one at a time in upload order.

What if the two files have different page counts?

Pages are interleaved in turn; the remaining pages of the longer file are added at the end.

I scanned the backs in reverse — what do I do?

Tick the option to read the second file in reverse order; the tool puts the backs in the right sequence.
