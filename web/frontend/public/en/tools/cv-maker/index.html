Resume & CV Builder That Checks Itself Against ATS Filters

Most resumes are read by software before a person sees them, so this builder is organised around that. Pick one of 30 templates, fill in the form, and the page redraws as you type. Then run the checks: the ATS X-ray opens your finished PDF the way an applicant-tracking system would and reports whether your name comes first, whether the email and phone are picked up, whether the headings are recognised and whether a multi-column layout is likely to scramble the reading order. Paste a job ad and the keyword matcher lists which of its terms your resume already uses and which are missing. A Fit-to-one-page option squeezes a long resume onto a single sheet, and empty sections never reach the PDF. Export as PDF with selectable text, as Word (.docx) or as plain text. Everything is assembled in your browser and the draft stays on your device, with room for up to 12 saved versions, which is handy when you keep one resume per role. A free account unlocks four templates; the remaining ones, the AI tab and the AI photo tools are Pro. Layouts range from a single plain column to sidebars, banners, timelines and dated-column designs in the spirit of Europass (not an official Europass file). Headings can be set in English or Turkish, and the content can be written in any language.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the ATS X-ray actually check?

It reads your exported PDF back as plain text, the way a hiring system would, and reports four things: whether your name is the first line, whether your email and phone number are recognised, whether your section headings are understood, and whether the page is multi-column. Multi-column templates trigger a warning; the Plain template gives the simplest reading order.

How do I match my resume to a specific job posting?

Paste the ad into the Analysis tab. You get a list of the ad's keywords that already appear in your resume and those that do not, and the comparison runs on your device. In the AI tab (Pro), "Tailor to a job ad" works on a separate copy, so your original stays intact and no numbers or facts that are not in your resume are invented.

Do I have to pay to download?

No payment is needed for the four templates included with a free account. The other templates are Pro; free users can open them faded with sample content, but not export them.

Why are some sections missing from my PDF?

On purpose. Empty fields show as faded placeholders in the preview so you can see what is available, but the PDF leaves out any empty field or section. Switch to PDF view to preview the exact result.

Should I put a photo on my resume?

That depends on the country and the employer, and it is optional here. You can drag and zoom a photo into place or leave it out entirely. If you add one, the AI Photo Studio can prepare it for the template's photo slot.

Can I get a Word file or reuse an older resume?

You can export PDF, Word (.docx) and plain text. To start from an old resume, import a PDF, Word or text file in the AI tab (Pro); scanned PDFs are read with on-device OCR, and the result is saved as a new entry rather than overwriting anything.

Is there a Europass-like layout?

Three templates place dates in a left column with details on the right: Europass Style, Dated Serif and Dated Teal. They imitate the look, not the official Europass file format.

Can it write a cover letter?

In the AI tab (Pro) you enter the posting, the company and the role, choose a tone and receive a draft. Edit it and download it as a PDF using the same design as your resume. Always review the draft before sending it.

Where is my data stored?

The resume is assembled in your browser and the draft lives only on this device; nothing is sent to our servers. The AI photo preparation also runs on your device.

How many pages will my resume be?

Pages are added as needed. A job entry or heading is never cut in half at a page break; it moves to the next page. Use Fit to one page if you want a single sheet.

Can I build a resume in English if I am in Turkey, or the reverse?

Yes. Choose the document language under Look & language: section headings and date formats follow it. The text you type can be in any language, and Turkish characters are embedded in the fonts.

Can I start from a blank template?

Yes. Download the blank template to get a PDF with placeholder text, then type over it with the Edit PDF tool.
