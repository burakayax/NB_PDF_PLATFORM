Fill a PDF Form — On Your Device, Private

Fill applications, contracts, consent and agency forms on your own computer. The moment you pick a file, its fields (text boxes, checkboxes, dropdowns) are detected and listed for you; fill them in and download. With «Lock after filling», the fields become permanent content so the recipient cannot change what you wrote. Everything happens in your browser — your document is NEVER uploaded, which matters for identity and application paperwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fill a PDF form?

Pick the form; fillable fields are detected and listed automatically. Fill them in, click 'Fill form' and download the file.

Does my file get uploaded?

No. Reading and filling happen entirely in your browser (on your device); the document is never uploaded.

Are accented characters preserved?

Yes. Field appearances are generated with a Unicode font, so characters outside the basic Latin set render correctly.

Can the filled fields be changed later?

Not if 'Lock after filling' is on: fields become permanent content. Leave it off to keep the form editable.

What if my PDF has no fillable fields?

Then it is a flat PDF. Use Edit PDF or Annotate PDF to write on top of it.

Are all PDF forms supported?

Standard PDF forms (AcroForm) are supported. Legacy Adobe LiveCycle (XFA) forms cannot be filled in a browser; the tool tells you when it sees one.
